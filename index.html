Quick guide

What matters most
Bark

Do not wrap just because the meat reaches a number. Wait until the bark is dark mahogany, fairly dry, and does not smear.

Sweet rubs

If your rub has a lot of brown sugar, wrap a little earlier once the bark is set to reduce the chance of scorching.

Finish

Start probing around 198°F. Most shoulders finish around 200–205°F, but tenderness is the real test.

Rest

Keep it wrapped and hold 1–2 hours minimum. A 2-hour hold is built into this schedule.
